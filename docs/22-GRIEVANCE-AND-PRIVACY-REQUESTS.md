# MAXERY — Grievance Redressal & Privacy Requests

## 1. Statutory Grievance Redressal Mechanism

Under Section 13 of the DPDPA 2023, every Data Principal has the right to readily available grievance redressal mechanisms provided by the Data Fiduciary regarding the performance of its obligations or the exercise of the Data Principal's rights.

### Operational Timelines:
- **Acknowledgement SLA:** All grievances submitted through the portal or official email must be acknowledged with a unique tracking ticket within **twenty-four (24) hours**.
- **Resolution SLA:** Formal investigation and substantive resolution must be provided to the Data Principal within **thirty (30) calendar days**.

---

## 2. Categories of Privacy Requests

Citizens may exercise their statutory rights through the self-service privacy portal or by submitting a ticket:

1. **Subject Access Request (`ACCESS_EXPORT`):**
   - Provides a comprehensive, machine-readable JSON export of all personal data, verified medical summaries, contact nominations, and an itemized log of all emergency responder lookups.
2. **Data Rectification (`RECTIFICATION`):**
   - Corrects inaccurate, misleading, or outdated medical information (e.g., updating asthma status or phone number).
3. **Erasure / Right to Be Forgotten (`ERASURE_FORGOTTEN`):**
   - Requests complete purging of all personal and medical information from active databases.
4. **Processing Restriction (`RESTRICTION`):**
   - Temporarily freezes emergency token lookup functionality while a dispute or identity verification is underway.
