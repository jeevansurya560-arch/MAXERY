## 1. Statutory Notice Framework (Section 5, DPDPA 2023)

Under Section 5 of the DPDPA 2023, every request for consent must be accompanied or preceded by an itemized notice provided in clear and plain language, available in English and specified regional Indian languages (Eighth Schedule).

### Notice Itemization Requirements:
1. The personal data to be collected.
2. The specific life-safety purpose of processing.
3. The manner in which the Data Principal may exercise their rights of access, correction, and withdrawal.
4. The procedure for filing a grievance with the Grievance Officer.

## 2. Standardized Consent Notice Templates

### Notice Template 1: Citizen Profile & Health Data Enrollment
```text
NOTICE AND CONSENT FOR EMERGENCY MEDICAL DATA PROCESSING

By registering with MAXERY, you consent to the collection, secure storage, and processing of your personal information (name, contact details, date of birth, blood group) and emergency medical constraints (drug allergies, chronic conditions, and medications).

PURPOSE:
This data is stored solely to allow accredited first responders, paramedics, and hospital emergency wards to quickly identify you, avoid fatal medical contraindications, and contact your nominated loved ones if you are incapacitated in an emergency.

ACCESS CONTROL:
Your physical emergency QR card encodes only a random, unguessable token and does NOT display your medical history. Only authenticated responders in an active incident may view your emergency summary. Every access is permanently logged with the responder's identity and timestamp.

RIGHTS & WITHDRAWAL:
You have the right to review your data, update medical notes, revoke physical QR tokens, or completely delete your account at any time through your Privacy Settings. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the legality of processing conducted prior to withdrawal.

GRIEVANCES:
For inquiries or complaints, contact grievance@maxery.org.in or the Data Protection Board of India.

[ ] I HAVE READ THIS NOTICE AND EXPLICITLY CONSENT TO THE PROCESSING OF MY EMERGENCY HEALTH DATA.
```

### Notice Template 2: Emergency Contact Information
```text
NOTICE FOR TRUSTED EMERGENCY CONTACT NOMINATION

You are registering an individual as a Trusted Emergency Contact.

By entering their details, you confirm that:
1. You have informed this individual that their name, phone number, and relationship have been nominated for emergency notification.
2. In the event you are incapacitated, authorized responders may contact them by phone to coordinate your care and notify them of your hospital location.
3. Their details will never be used for marketing, automated sales, or third-party sharing.
```
